How to play

  • Start: choose an image (or drop one onto the page), then a difficulty or a piece count from the same menu (a piece count keeps your "Rotate pieces" setting; "Custom…" takes any number from 4 to 500). The image stays on your device.
  • Several pictures: select more than one image when choosing (or drop several, or use "Choose a folder…" in Options on a computer) and one is picked at random. "Next picture" starts a puzzle from another one, going through them all before any repeats; a solved picture is taken out of the set, so it doesn't come up again.
  • Sample: no picture handy? (replaces the current puzzle).
  • Move: drag a piece with the mouse, a finger, or a pen.
  • Join: drop a piece close to a matching neighbor and they snap together, then move as a group.
  • Lock: drop a piece or group near its spot inside the dashed outline and it locks onto the board. With "Edges first" on, only edge pieces (or groups that include one) lock in by themselves; an inner piece has to join a matching piece already in place.
  • Rotate (when "Rotate pieces" is on): right-click or double-tap a piece, or press R or Space while dragging. Hold Shift (with right-click, R or Space) to turn the other way. Pieces only join when turned the same way, and only lock when upright.
  • Zoom: scroll the mouse wheel over the table, pinch with two fingers, or use the − / + buttons; click the percentage to zoom back out. While zoomed in, drag an empty spot to look around.
  • Shuffle: scatters the loose pieces and groups to new spots around the board, without re-cutting.
  • Gather edges: moves the loose border pieces together beside the board.
  • Options: the Options menu holds Edges first, Sound, Preview and the table color.
  • Preview (in Options): choose no preview, a faint ghost of the picture on the board, or a small copy of the whole picture in the corner (click it for a large view; click again to close).
  • New cut: re-cuts the same image with fresh piece shapes.
  • Menu: the ▴ button at the top left folds the menu bar away to leave more room for the table (handy on phones); ▾ brings it back.
  • Saving: your puzzle is saved in this browser as you play, and comes back when you return.
