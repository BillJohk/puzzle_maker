How to play

  • Start: choose an image (or drop one onto the page), then a difficulty or your own piece count ("Custom…" takes any number from 4 to 500). The image stays on your device.
  • Move: drag a piece with the mouse, a finger, or a pen.
  • Join: drop a piece close to a matching neighbor and they snap together, then move as a group.
  • Lock: drop a piece or group near its spot inside the dashed outline and it locks onto the board.
  • Rotate (when "Rotate pieces" is on): right-click or double-tap a piece, or press R or Space while dragging. Pieces only join when turned the same way, and only lock when upright.
  • Zoom: scroll the mouse wheel over the table, pinch with two fingers, or use the − / + buttons; click the percentage to zoom back out. While zoomed in, drag an empty spot to look around.
  • Shuffle: scatters the loose pieces and groups to new spots around the board, without re-cutting.
  • Gather edges: moves the loose border pieces together beside the board.
  • Preview: cycles between no preview, a faint ghost of the picture on the board, and a small copy of the whole picture in the corner.
  • New cut: re-cuts the same image with fresh piece shapes.
  • Saving: your puzzle is saved in this browser as you play, and comes back when you return.
